About

An ephemeris is a calculated table of celestial positions over time—self-contained data that allows navigators to fix their coordinates from first principles without pinging a remote source.

Ephemeris Labs designs software under that same philosophy: completely deterministic, privacy-focused, and executed entirely on local hardware without cloud or connectivity dependencies.
