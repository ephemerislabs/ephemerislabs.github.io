How we work

An ephemeris is a table of where things will be — the kind navigators steer by. It is only useful if it is right. That is the standard for everything we build.

Primary sources. Every figure comes from the authority that publishes it, cited, and is checked against that source before it ships.

Built from inside. We build for fields we work in ourselves — by people who live under the rules the software models, for the people it is about.

Yours, on your device. No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads. Your data stays with you; back it up to a file you keep.

Paid once. A fair price, no subscriptions, and a standing offer on accuracy: find a true bug or a wrong figure in anything we make, be the first to report it, and we pay you the price of the app.

Plain words. The forms and the pay tables are complicated enough. Our apps say what they mean.
