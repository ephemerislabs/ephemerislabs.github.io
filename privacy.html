Privacy PolicyDRILL WIZARD · EPHEMERIS LABS LLC

The short version

Drill Wizard collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no identifiers. Everything you enter — your grade, duty ledger, VA details, projections — is stored in the app's storage on your device and goes nowhere else.

What stays on your device

All profile data, scheduled and logged duty, reconciliation entries, imports, and settings live only in local app storage. We never see them. Backups exist only when you export one, as a file you control.

When the app touches the network

Two optional actions, both started by you:

Rate updates. "Check for rate updates" fetches static rate files (pay tables, VA rates, per diem) from the app's own site. This is an ordinary web request; it carries no personal data.

LES scan (optional beta). The first scan downloads an open-source OCR engine (~3 MB) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, a public content network, which sees a standard web request from your device. Your images are processed on the device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera or photo access only if you choose to scan an LES, and only to hand the image to on-device processing.

Deleting your data

Erase everything in-app (Profile → Data → erase), or delete the app — local storage goes with it. There is nothing to delete on our side, because nothing was collected.

Selling or sharing

We do not sell, share, or monetize personal data. There is none to sell.

Children

Drill Wizard is a financial planning tool for serving Guard and Reserve members and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Questions: support@ephemerislabs.app.

Drill Wizard is a planning tool, not financial, tax, or legal advice, and is not affiliated with the DoD, VA, or DFAS.
