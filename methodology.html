Points & participation (statute-locked)

1 point per IDT period and per day on orders; 15 membership points per anniversary year (DoDI 1215.07). The 130-point inactive cap includes membership (10 U.S.C. §12733). A good year is 50 points — the app calls it secured only when the points are banked, never on scheduled duty. The AT tracker defaults to the statutory 14 days (10 U.S.C. §10147), unit-tunable. These change only on statutory amendment, never on a calendar.

Verification

Each pack build is spot-verified against the primary source — table cells checked to the cent, not to the summary. The August 2026 audit re-verified DFAS basic-pay anchors, both BAS figures, the full VA.gov dependent-tier cells, FSA/IDP amounts, and caught and corrected the July 2025 SGLI rate cut. The app ships with a 500+-check regression harness; money math is computed once, in one engine, so no two cards can disagree.

What the app deliberately does not do

It does not model Special Monthly Compensation, the Social Security wage base, or Additional Medicare tax; it treats TSP contributions with post-tax (Roth-style) arithmetic, which slightly understates in-pocket cash for traditional contributions — a deliberately conservative choice; it scopes combat-zone tax relief to the order being priced; and it never invents service-schedule pay amounts — those are entered from your LES. Where its numbers and the government's differ, your LES, pay system, and official VA correspondence win.
