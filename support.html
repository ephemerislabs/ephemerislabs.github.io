The accuracy bounty

This app's job is to be right. Find a true bug or an inaccurate figure, report it so we can fix it, and Ephemeris Labs will pay you the price of the app. First report wins: one bounty per defect, paid to whoever reports it first, once we confirm it against the source it comes from — your LES, the published rate table, or the statute.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your phone. There is no account and no server. Use Settings → Data → Download profile to save a copy to Files; Upload profile restores it on a new phone.
  • The rates look old. The app checks for updated rate tables once a day when it opens. Settings → Check for rate updates runs the check now. The Rates tab shows every table's effective date and whether it has been superseded.
  • The LES photo didn't read. Straight on, good light, the whole remarks section in frame. The first use downloads a small on-device reader (~3 MB), so use Wi-Fi once. Images never leave your phone.
  • My unit's muster email isn't recognized. The muster reader knows the Navy's format today. Send a screenshot of your unit's muster email to support and we will add yours.
  • A number differs from my LES. Your LES, NSIPS or myPay, and official VA correspondence win. Tell us and we will find out why.

Fine print

Drill Wizard estimates; it does not advise. Figures come from published official rate tables, but nothing in the app is financial, tax, or legal advice. Drill Wizard is not a DoD, VA, or DFAS system and is not affiliated with any government agency.
