Where is my data?

On your device, nowhere else. There's no account and no server. That also means we can't recover it for you — export a backup from Profile → Data whenever your ledger matters.

How do I move to a new phone?

Old phone: Profile → Data → Export backup. New phone: Import backup. The file is yours the whole way.

Are the rates current?

Military pay refreshes every 1 January and VA rates every 1 December. Tap Profile → "Check for rate updates" after each. The app names its data pack (e.g. CY2026) on the home screen.

My 21-8951 count doesn't match the app

Enter the form's pre-printed count on the recon card — the app prices the difference and stages the evidence ledger. Days on orders ride a separate pay pipeline from drills, which is the usual source of surprise counts. The app is planning support, not an official record: LESs, orders, and your points record are the evidence that decides it.

How do I erase everything?

Profile → Data → erase, or delete the app. Both remove all local data permanently.
