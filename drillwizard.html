What it does

  • Three calendars, kept straight. Points and good years by anniversary year, pay and taxes by calendar year, the VA waiver by fiscal year.
  • Priced like DFAS. Basic ÷ 30 per drill period, FICA and TSP on basic pay only, the BRS match — and orders line by line: BAH, BAS, per diem, quarters, CZTE.
  • Reads your LES. Photograph it; the app reads what was paid and checks it against your plan. Muster emails answer your check-in.
  • Sees the 21-8951 coming. Projected waiver days, the payback, what to set aside each month — before the form mails.
  • Retirement, honestly. Points, good years, earliest retired pay with the early-retirement credit, retired pay, TSP at pay start, and a goal-seek that solves the drilling pace for an income target.
